
# 📊 Enterprise Customer Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **enterprise customer data** to identify key revenue drivers, high-value customers, and regional performance patterns.
The goal is to convert raw transactional data into **actionable business insights** using **Python for analysis** and **Power BI for visualization**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify **top revenue-generating customers**
* Analyze **revenue distribution across countries**
* Understand **customer contribution to overall revenue**
* Create an **interactive, business-friendly dashboard**
* Practice a **realistic analytics workflow** used in industry

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* **Python** (Pandas) – Data cleaning & analysis
* **Power BI** – Data modeling & dashboard visualization
* **GitHub** – Version control & project documentation

---

## 📂 Dataset

* Enterprise customer transactional dataset
* Includes customer details, country, order quantity, and revenue
* Dataset was cleaned to remove missing values and inconsistencies

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Cleaning**

   * Removed null and invalid records
   * Standardized column formats
   * Verified revenue calculations

2. **Data Analysis**

   * Calculated total revenue per customer
   * Ranked customers by revenue contribution
   * Analyzed revenue by country

3. **Dashboard Development**

   * KPI cards for total revenue and customers
   * Bar charts for **Top Customers by Revenue**
   * Country-wise revenue analysis
---

## 📊 Key Insights

* A **small percentage of customers contribute a large share of revenue**
* Certain countries dominate overall enterprise revenue
* Customer ranking helps identify **high-value enterprise clients**
